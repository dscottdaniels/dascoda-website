PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 23, 2026

How we handle information collected through dascoda.com.

Dascoda Solutions ("Dascoda," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to handling information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect when you visit dascoda.com, contact us through the website, or otherwise interact with us online, and how we may use and protect that information.

Information You Provide to Us

When you contact Dascoda through our website, email, or other communication channels, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, company, email address, phone number, and information related to your inquiry.

We use this information to respond to your request, communicate with you, evaluate potential business opportunities, provide information about our services, and support our business relationship with you.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through our website contact form.

Website and Usage Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, such as your browser type, device type, referring website, pages visited, approximate geographic region, and information about how visitors interact with the site.

We may use website analytics technologies, including Google Analytics, to help us understand how visitors find and use our website and to improve our content, services, and website experience.

Analytics information is intended to be used in aggregate and for website measurement purposes. Dascoda does not intentionally send information submitted through contact forms, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or message content, to Google Analytics.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and service providers may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand website usage, measure traffic, and evaluate the effectiveness of our online content.

Browser settings may allow you to control or delete cookies. Certain privacy choices may also be available depending on your browser, device, and location.

Contact Form Processing

Dascoda currently uses a third-party form-processing service to transmit information submitted through our website contact form. Information submitted through the form may therefore be processed by that service for the purpose of delivering your inquiry to Dascoda.

We encourage visitors not to submit confidential, proprietary, financial, health, or other sensitive information through the website contact form.

How We Use Information

We may use information collected through the website to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Communicate with customers and prospective customers
  • Provide and improve our services
  • Understand website usage and performance
  • Maintain website security and reliability
  • Support business operations
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

How We Share Information

Dascoda does not sell personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our website, process communications, provide analytics, host technology, or support our business operations. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect our rights or the rights of others, investigate misuse or security concerns, or respond to lawful requests.

We do not authorize service providers to use personal information provided to them on our behalf for purposes unrelated to the services they provide to Dascoda, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, support legitimate business needs, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal or contractual obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

Data Security

Dascoda uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information under our control. However, no website, electronic transmission, or information system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties, including technology providers, partners, and social media platforms. Dascoda is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies when visiting those services.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights regarding your personal information, which may include the ability to request access, correction, deletion, or other information about how personal information is handled.

You may contact Dascoda with a privacy-related request through our Contact page. We may need to verify your request before responding.

Children's Privacy

Dascoda's website and services are intended for businesses and professionals and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our website, services, technologies, business practices, or legal requirements. When we make changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Dascoda's privacy practices, please contact us through our Contact page.
